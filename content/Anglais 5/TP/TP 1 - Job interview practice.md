# TP 1 — Job interview practice

**Goal:** practise a full internship interview in English, in pairs, using the phrases from [[Chapter 1 - Professional communication]].

## Part 1 — Prepare (20 min)

- [ ] Choose a real internship offer written in English (LinkedIn, company career pages) and underline five key requirements
- [ ] Write your **"Tell me about yourself"** answer (about 150 words): present → past → future
- [ ] Prepare one **STAR** story about a difficulty in a project
- [ ] Prepare two questions to ask the interviewer

> [!exemple] "Tell me about yourself" — a model answer
> *I'm a final-year computer science student. Over the past two years, I've focused on web and cloud development: for example, I built a REST API with Spring Boot and deployed it with Docker for a student club. I enjoy solving problems as part of a team, and I'm now looking for an internship where I can work on real products and learn from experienced engineers. That's why your position caught my attention.*

## Part 2 — Role play (2 × 15 min)

Work in pairs. Student A is the interviewer and Student B the candidate; then swap.

The interviewer's questions:

1. *Tell me about yourself.*
2. *Why are you interested in this internship?*
3. *Describe a project you are proud of. What was your role?*
4. *Tell me about a time you had a problem in a team. What did you do?*
5. *What is your biggest weakness?*
6. *Explain to me, as if I weren't technical, what an API is.*
7. *Do you have any questions for us?*

- [ ] The interviewer takes notes using the feedback grid below
- [ ] The candidate records the interview on their phone (optional, but very useful)

## Part 3 — Feedback (10 min)

- [ ] Give your partner **two strengths** and **one thing to improve**
- [ ] Write down three mistakes you made and their correction (check the [[Résumé - Anglais professionnel|common mistakes table]])

## Part 4 — Follow-up email (homework)

- [ ] Write a short thank-you email (80–120 words) to the interviewer, to be sent the day after the interview
